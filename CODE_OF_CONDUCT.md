# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
publishing another person's private information, sexualized conduct, and other conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Never post credentials, account identifiers, private market data, exploit details, or other
sensitive material in a public issue. Follow `SECURITY.md` for private vulnerability reports.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not
align with this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior through a private GitHub
security advisory. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as is
practical. Maintainers who are the subject of a report must not participate in its handling.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.
